Who We Are

Organization Background

Building a Brighter Future for Tanzanian Youth

Tanzanian Youth and Children (TYC) is a membership non-profit making development-oriented local organization, initially established in 2017 as a Community Based Organization (CBO) and finally registered as an NGO in 2022 with registration number 00NGO/R/3052.

TYC is established by a group of concerned voluntary Tanzanians who envisioned seeing children and youth access better, quality education and enrich their full potentials.

Main Goal: To improve access to inclusive, quality, and skill-focused education for Tanzanian youth and children in underserved communities.

Vision

A Tanzania where every child and youth is empowered with quality education, creativity, and skills to thrive and contribute to national development.

Mission

To work with communities, educators, and stakeholders to provide inclusive, child-centered, and skill-based education that prepares Tanzania's young people for future opportunities.

Our Interventions

Key Organization Activities

Component A: Learning Environments

Supply textbooks, STEM kits, and educational materials.
Construct and renovate classrooms, libraries, and sanitation facilities.

Component B: Teacher Development

Train teachers in learner-centered and inclusive pedagogy.
Provide ICT and digital teaching tool workshops.

Component C: Digital Literacy & Tech

Set up ICT labs with computers and internet access.
Offer digital skills training: coding, digital marketing, robotics.
Run holiday tech camps for youth.

Component D: Youth Empowerment

Mentor programs linking students with professionals.
Leadership, communication, and financial literacy training.
Career guidance and entrepreneurship workshops.

Component E: Community Engagement

Awareness campaigns on the importance of education.
Training for parents and community leaders on child protection.
Establish or strengthen community education committees.
